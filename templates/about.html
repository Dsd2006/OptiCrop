OUR VISION

Growing a Smarter Future

OptiCrop combines artificial intelligence and agriculture to help farmers make informed crop selection decisions using soil nutrients and environmental conditions.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

What is OptiCrop?

OptiCrop is an AI-powered smart agriculture application developed using Python, Flask and Machine Learning. It uses soil and weather parameters to predict suitable crops through a trained Random Forest classification model.

Our Mission

Our mission is to demonstrate how data-driven technology can support agricultural decision-making and encourage smarter farming practices.

Technology Behind OptiCrop

Python & Flask

Backend development and web application management.

Machine Learning

Random Forest classification for crop recommendations.

SQLite Database

Storage of generated crop prediction history.

How It Works

1. Enter soil nutrient values: Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium.
2. Provide temperature, humidity, pH and rainfall information.
3. The trained AI model analyses the input data.
4. OptiCrop displays a predicted crop and model confidence.
5. The prediction is stored for dashboard tracking.

Our Goal

To showcase the potential of AI in agriculture through an easy-to-use, informative and modern digital platform. Predictions should be treated as decision-support suggestions and verified with local agricultural expertise.
